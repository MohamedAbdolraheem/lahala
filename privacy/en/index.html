In short

Lahala keeps your spending on your iPhone, and in your own iCloud if you turn on sync. We have no servers, no accounts and no analytics, and we never see your messages, amounts or card numbers.

What Lahala reads

Lahala reads only the bank messages you send to it, in one of three ways:

  • The automation you create in the Shortcuts app, which passes each bank message containing “SAR”, or another currency word your bank uses, such as “SR”, that Lahala notices in your messages and you add when it suggests it, to Lahala on your iPhone.
  • The “Lahala Past Payments” shortcut, when you run it to add earlier payments.
  • A message you paste into the app yourself.

Each message is read on your iPhone, and Lahala keeps only the payment details it finds. Messages that aren't payments, such as verification codes and offers, are ignored and not stored.

A salary deposit message is not logged as a payment: Lahala keeps only the day it arrived, to know when your month starts, and never its amount or text.

What it stores, and where

On your iPhone, Lahala stores:

  • The amount and currency, the merchant or recipient name, the category, and the date and time.
  • The payment method, and the last 4 digits of the card or account when the message includes them.
  • The name of the message's sender, such as “alinma”.
  • What you add: notes, category changes, your monthly income and payday, and the monthly payments you confirm.
  • Your challenges and their results, and your Worth it? answers about your purchases.
  • The questions you pin in Ask Lahala, whose answers show in Ask Lahala and in the Home Screen widget.
  • The days your salary message arrived (the last 6 only), the currency words you added, and when the automation last sent messages, so Lahala can tell you if it stops.

Lahala does not store the text of your messages.

This data stays in Lahala's private storage on your iPhone, which Lahala's Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read from. It is not uploaded anywhere unless you turn on Sync with iCloud. If you back up your iPhone, it is part of that backup, with Apple or on your computer.

Sync with iCloud

Sync is optional and off at first. When you turn on “Sync with iCloud” in Settings, Lahala syncs your data between your devices through your own private iCloud database, in your account. We can't read it.

  • Synced: transactions, including the card or account last 4 digits and the sender name; rules; monthly payments; Worth it? answers; your monthly income, payday, salary days and what you'd spent before you started; pinned questions; and challenges.
  • Kept on this iPhone only: the automation's status and alerts, the currency words you added, your setup progress, and your notification switches.
  • Turning it off: turning sync off, or turning iCloud off for Lahala, stops syncing. What was already uploaded stays in your iCloud until you delete it in your iPhone's Settings.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No servers: Lahala never sends your data to us or anyone else. Sync, if you turn it on, goes only to your own iCloud.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party libraries.
  • No selling or sharing your data with anyone.
  • Fixed rules: Lahala reads your messages with fixed rules on your iPhone, and uses them to calculate every number and Ask Lahala answer from your own payments.

Notifications

All of Lahala's notifications are scheduled on your iPhone. Their Lock Screen text never contains amounts, merchant names or message text.

  • The weekly recap, every Saturday morning.
  • A reminder 2 days before your free trial ends, if you ask for it.
  • Automation alerts: when the sender's name arrives instead of the message, or no bank messages arrive for several days.
  • Lahala Pro alerts, such as challenges, money running out before payday, and a monthly payment you confirmed coming due.

You're in control

  • Export: in Settings you can export your transactions as a CSV file through the share sheet, and you choose where it goes. The file contains personal data, so share it with care.
  • Delete: “Delete all data” in Settings erases transactions, rules and settings, challenges, Worth it? answers and pinned questions from your iPhone. With sync on, it also deletes them from iCloud and all your devices.
  • Uninstall: deleting the app deletes all of its data from your iPhone.

Apple services

Shortcuts runs on your iPhone. Sync with iCloud, downloading Lahala, its updates, and a Lahala Pro subscription if you choose one, are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we receive no payment details.

Saudi Personal Data Protection Law

Lahala is designed on the principles of the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law: as little data as possible, used for one purpose, showing you your spending, and fully under your control.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date, and mention any significant change in the app's update notes.
