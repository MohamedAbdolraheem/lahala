Common questions

How do I turn on automatic logging?

Open Lahala, then Settings → Set up automatic logging, and follow the steps and the video. You create one automation in the Shortcuts app, once per iPhone.

I paid, but the payment didn't appear

  • In the automation, choose Run Immediately, not Run After Confirmation.
  • The trigger is Message Contains with exactly SAR.
  • In Log Transaction, the message is Shortcut Input.
  • After restarting your iPhone, unlock it once before payments are logged.
  • If your bank isn't supported yet, send us a sample message (see below).

How do I add my earlier payments?

In Settings → Past messages, tap “Add the import shortcut” to add the “Lahala Past Payments” shortcut, then “Import past SMS”. Running the import again never logs a payment twice.

I got a refund, but it doesn't show

Some banks send refunds only as a notification from their app, with no SMS, and Lahala can't read those. Open the transaction and tap “Mark as refunded”.

How do I send a sample message from my bank?

Copy the message, replace card and account numbers with 0000, remove any person's name, and email it to us. We add banks from real messages only.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Everything stays on your iPhone, and we never receive your messages or amounts. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete all data. Or delete the app, which deletes all of its data from your iPhone.
